### Team Decisions

**1. Website Structure**
- Single-page website (agreed by all team members)
- Reasoning: faster to build and easier to keep consistent than a multi-page site

**2. Images**
- Each member uses their own photos
- Additional photos of personal hobbies will be included alongside member profiles
- All photos should follow a consistent aspect ratio and orientation (e.g. avoid mixing full-body shots with close-ups) to keep the layout uniform across all member cards

**3. Team Member Sections**
- Each member builds and owns their own section (rather than profiling a teammate)
- Reasoning: time constraints, some team members working full-time alongside the course

**4. Extra Feature**
- A "Fun Fact" section will be added to each member's profile, in addition to the required bio, skills, and hobbies

**5. Build Approach**
- A shared template will be built first (header, footer, styling, layout structure)
- Each team member will then build their own section on top of the shared template
- This keeps styling and structure consistent across all sections without needing constant coordination
